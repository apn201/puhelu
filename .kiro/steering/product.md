# Product

Puhelu is a haunted phone line. An old telephone rings at night. Whoever answers lands in the phone menu of Thirdwatch Customer Continuity, a fictional company, run by a storylet director that remembers across calls and nights. Hang up and it calls back.

Two front ends, one engine:
- The hardware: Arduino UNO Q + Silvertel AG1171 SLIC + a 1950s Swedish Ericofon (never opened or modified).
- The hosted web phone: rotary dial and keypad in the browser. Judges use this one.

Arc: night 1 polite. Night 2 remembers (flags, counts). Night 3 and later a human agent appears, tries to help, and is cut off by the robot. The human's advice (dial four) is the only exit.

What the haunting knows comes only from its own state: calls, hang-ups, silences, dial speed, hesitation. Nothing outside the box.

Judging: Potential Impact, Technological Implementation, Design, Innovation/Idea, Presentation.
